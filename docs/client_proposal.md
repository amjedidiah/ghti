# **GHTI Website & Student Portal Proposal**

**Date:** November 14, 2026 (revised November 15, 2026)  
**Prepared For:** GHTI, Charismatic Renewal Ministries
**Prepared By:** Jedidiah Amaraegbu

## **1. Summary**

A website and student portal for GHTI at **ghti.crm-world.org.ng**, using the same CRM header and footer as the other subdomains. It covers your Certificate, Diploma and Degree programmes, from application to graduation.

## **2. What You Get**

- **Website:** welcome banner, vision, history, Statement of Faith, programmes with downloadable lecture schedules, contact form, phone numbers, addresses and map.
- **Online Admissions:** application form with biodata and transcript upload; admin review, admit or reject; email updates.
- **Student Portal:** records, published grades, GPA, academic history, course videos and materials.
- **Lecturer Portal:** add video and material links, enter grades.
- **Admin Dashboard:** search and update student files, approve grades, manage programmes and site text. Every grade change is logged.
- **Certificates:** each award has a QR code linking to a public verification page.

## **3. Timeline (12 Weeks)**

We build in three stages. Each stage is a working system on a live link that you can use and give feedback on, so you see progress from the first week instead of waiting until the end.

- **Stage 1, Weeks 1-4: Website and online applications.** Visitors browse the site and programmes; applicants create an account, apply and upload their transcript; admins review, admit or reject.
  - _Week 1 milestone: look-and-feel approval_ on a live preview link.
  - _Week 4 milestone: applications open_ for your first intake.
- **Stage 2, Weeks 5-8: Student records and results.** Admitted applicants become students with matric numbers; admins enter and publish results; students sign in to see their grades and GPA; admins search and update student files.
  - _Milestone: a term's results published online._
- **Stage 3, Weeks 9-12: Lecturers, courses and certificates.** Lecturers add videos and materials and enter grades for approval; certificates with QR verification; unofficial transcripts; staff training.
  - _Milestone: full launch._
